## About me
I graduated from the [University of Chicago](https://biologicalsciences.uchicago.edu/) in 2022 with a B.A. in Genetics. I then worked as a bioinformatician in the [Moskowitz Lab](https://moskowitzlab.uchicago.edu/) at the University of Chicago, where I used genomic/bioinformatic approaches (RNA-seq, ATAC-seq, and ChIP-seq) to study the gene regulatory networks governing the timing of cell fate determination during mammalian cardiogenesis and neurogenesis.

I am an incoming doctoral student at [New York University](https://as.nyu.edu/departments/biology/academics/phd.html), starting in Fall 2026.
